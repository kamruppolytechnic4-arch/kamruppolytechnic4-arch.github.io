Affiliate disclosure

Colour does not sell anything. Every product is bought on the seller’s own site, and each “Buy from” link opens there in a new tab. Some of those links may earn Colour a commission in future. Click analytics are off by default; when the local runtime is configured, it records only the product, seller, link placement and time, without cookies or browser fingerprints. Commission never affects which products are listed or how they are scored: placement is decided only by verified colour accuracy, material and construction.
